Domain Registration Strategy & DNS Architecture

Your domain name is your business's permanent asset. Discover how to choose memorable names, navigate TLD rankings, and claim a free domain without registrar markups.

1. The 4 Golden Rules of High-Converting Domain Names

  • Prioritize .com when possible: Over 48% of global internet traffic defaults to typing .com. If taken, prefer clean prefixes like get, use, join over hyphenated variations.
  • Pass the Radio Test: If you say your domain on a podcast or over the phone, can the listener spell it without explanation? Avoid homophones and odd spellings.
  • Zero Hyphens or Numbers: Hyphens (e.g. best-host-review.com) look like low-quality spam to users and trigger higher bounce rates.
  • Aim for 2 to 3 Syllables: Concise names like Stripe, Hostinger, Vercel stick in human memory far better than keyword-stuffed strings.

2. Never Pay for WHOIS Privacy Protection

When registering a domain, ICANN mandates public registration records. Without WHOIS privacy, your personal phone number, home address, and email are scraped by spammers within minutes.
