1. The Redis Factor: Why WooCommerce Stores Require Business

When a visitor navigates a typical blog post, LiteSpeed serves a static HTML cached file in 40ms without querying MySQL. However, when a customer views an e-commerce shopping cart, filters product attributes, or proceeds to checkout, page caching is completely bypassed.

On Hostinger Premium, every single un-cached checkout query hits the MySQL disk database. On Hostinger Business, the integrated Redis Object Cache intercepts database requests and serves frequent SQL queries directly from RAM via the local Unix domain socket:

# In wp-config.php on Hostinger Business:
define('WP_REDIS_SCHEME', 'unix');
define('WP_REDIS_PATH', '/var/run/redis/redis.sock');

2. Daily vs. Weekly Backups: What Happens When an Update Breaks?

WordPress plugins frequently push updates that conflict with PHP or your active theme.

  • On Hostinger Premium: Automated backups run only once every 7 days. If a plugin breaks your site on Friday and your last backup was Monday, restoring forces you to lose 4 days of new comments, orders, and post revisions.
  • On Hostinger Business: Backups run automatically every 24 hours with a rolling 30-day retention calendar. Crucially, Business also includes On-Demand Snapshots: you click "Generate Backup" immediately before updating WooCommerce or Elementor, giving you an instantaneous rollback safety net.
