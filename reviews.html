2. What Generic Hosting Reviews Hide: Inode Limits & Entry Processes

Almost every generic affiliate review claims Hostinger gives you "unlimited websites and unmetered storage." This marketing phrasing is technically misleading. In actual Linux server administration, your storage is constrained by two critical hidden metrics: Inodes and Entry Processes (EP).

A. The Inode Threshold Reality

An inode represents a single file, image, folder, or email stored on the server. Even if you only use 8 GB of your 100 GB NVMe storage quota, your account will freeze if you exhaust your inode allocation.

Hostinger's Actual Inode Allocations:
• Hostinger Premium ($2.99/mo): 400,000 Inodes (Sufficient for ~15–20 medium WordPress sites)
• Hostinger Business ($3.99/mo): 600,000 Inodes (Sufficient for ~35–50 sites or 1 heavy WooCommerce store)
• Hostinger Cloud Startup ($7.99/mo): 2,000,000 Inodes (Generous agency capacity)

Insider Advice: Avoid installing 10 different backup plugins that store uncompressed .tar.gz archives inside wp-content/uploads, as this rapidly consumes inodes. Use Hostinger's free automated daily backups instead.

B. Entry Processes (EP) vs. CPU Percentage

When a visitor requests a page that requires PHP execution (such as adding an item to a WooCommerce cart or logging into wp-admin), one PHP-FPM Entry Process is created. On standard Apache hosts (like Bluehost), slow scripts hang, creating backlogs that trigger 503 "Service Unavailable" errors.

Because Hostinger utilizes LiteSpeed's event-driven architecture, each PHP worker finishes execution in ~20–40ms, returning to the worker pool up to 5x faster than Apache. This enables Hostinger's 30 concurrent EP limit on Premium (and 60 on Business) to comfortably handle surges of up to 400 concurrent active shoppers without crashing.
