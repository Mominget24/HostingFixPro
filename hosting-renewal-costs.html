The 3 Dirty Tactics of Web Hosting Billing

1. The SSL Certificate Ransom Certain legacy registrars give you a free SSL certificate in Year 1, but silently enroll your account in auto-billing at $79.99 to $99.99/year thereafter. If you cancel, your website immediately shows a red "Dangerous Site Ahead" warning in Chrome. Hostinger uses automated Let's Encrypt certificates that renew automatically at $0.00 for the life of your account.
2. The 12-Month Renewal Short-Circuit Choosing a 1-year contract seems safe, but it cuts your savings short. In months 13 to 36, you pay full renewal price. Choosing a 48-month term locks your introductory rate across four years, shielding your budget against annual price inflation.
3. WHOIS Domain Privacy Fees Registrars often charge $9.99/year for WHOIS privacy protection. Without it, your personal contact numbers and email are publicly exposed. Hostinger includes lifetime WHOIS privacy free with every eligible domain registration.
